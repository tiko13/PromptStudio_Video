Video director discusses the whole project; the Shot director in a Shot Editor works on that shot. The Director provides reviewable proposals: a discussion or help answer does not change the project. Review the proposed changes before applying them.
Use Add shot on the Timeline for another shot; drag shots to reorder or resize their boundaries. Prompt in the top bar previews the compiled generation prompt. The structured document remains authoritative. Structured extensions use the per-shot Director; the project Video director is unavailable there.
