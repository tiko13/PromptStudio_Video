Use Install workflows in the top bar to choose a bundle, review its required download size and install it. Downloads resume and verify integrity; existing workflows are never overwritten. Select an installed workflow in the workflow picker. Original Turbo graphs keep their legacy policy.
Prompt Studio MiniMax H3 Sampling Profile (PSV_MiniMaxH3SamplingProfile) takes model, mode, width and height from the Director plus preset and attention. Its model, steps and shifts feed MiniMaxH3SigmaShift and BasicScheduler; sampler feeds SamplerCustomAdvanced. BasicScheduler must use simple, except TaoMate: keep its bundled ManualSigmas training grid. The node resolves the required acceleration LoRA and applies its coupled settings at strength 1. Keep content LoRAs separate.
Fast and Balanced use versioned FL adapters for base modes and a dedicated reference adapter for REF2VA. Full quality uses no acceleration adapter. Read the resolved profile display for current steps and shifts; REF and FL shifts differ. All use a PyTorch dense attention baseline. The sparse experiment keeps audio rows exact and may stay dense on short clips. Its native kernel must be available. FastH3 needs its matching VSA checkpoint and excludes REF2VA. TaoMate is restricted to text-to-video. Experimental presets need local comparisons of motion, identity, speech and timing before use for final output; speed gains are not guaranteed.
