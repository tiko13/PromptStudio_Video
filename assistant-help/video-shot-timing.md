Double-click a shot to open its Shot Editor. Overlapping events occupy separate rows. Select a cue to enter its local Start/End; drag its body to move or edges to resize. Arrow keys move; Alt+Arrow trims the end; Ctrl+Arrow trims the start. Use automatic flow removes authored cue timing. Exact-audio time fields use seconds; Source in/out, fades, gain and mix mode affect post-render mixing. Short audio sources with known duration can show a source waveform.
Shot Editor Undo/Redo is separate from project history until Save shot. Cancel discards the draft. History is session-only. Timing is local to the shot; its offset locates it in the project. Generated cue timing influences the model; exact audio is positioned by the post-render mixer.
