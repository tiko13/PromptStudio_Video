The Director plans shot structure before writing a proposal, then checks the result against your request and scene continuity. Sitting, standing and walking with a following camera normally form one continuous shot. Cinematic style or a new action does not by itself request a cut. Ask explicitly for multiple shots, a montage, cut times or editorial freedom when wanted; discrete requested scenes/time jumps may also imply cuts. Localized edits preserve existing shots and cut times. The Shot director cannot restructure the whole video.
Planning carries identity, reference roles, wardrobe, setting, props, movement direction and relevant object state forward. Explicitly requested scene or appearance changes remain allowed. First/last frame constraints, protected dialogue and visible text still apply. Extensions plan only the new tail from the source ending.
Progress distinguishes planning, authoring, continuity review and correction. Invalid structure or material continuity problems trigger bounded correction attempts; an unresolved result has no Apply proposal. Retry a failed request using its retained conversation. Planning and semantic review use the shared LLM and may take longer; they improve proposal checks but do not guarantee rendered continuity. Review the numbered shot cards and proposed cuts before applying. Planning metadata is not inserted into the generation prompt.
